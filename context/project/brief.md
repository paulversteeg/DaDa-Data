# Project brief

## Who is this for?

The show is written for the ensemble members of Jubal Indoor Percussion. Audiences visit and watch the show, and at performances a jury team adjudicates it.

## What problem do we solve?

There is no problem to solve. The show exists to be fun to rehearse and perform, and to entertain the audience.

- The difficulty sits a little above the ensemble's comfort level, so members can grow.
- The program is clear and comprehensible on the surface.
- Beneath the surface there is a deeper meaning and several levels of complexity, for audience members and jury teams who want to look closer.

## What is in scope, and what is not?

**In scope:**

- Music
- Drill
- Visual design
- Costumes
- Props
- Storyline
- Marketing material
- Planning: the rehearsal schedule and logistics

**Out of scope:** not provided yet.

**Priorities:** The main goal is to make music and to enjoy the rehearsals. There is a competition element, but it is secondary.

## How do we know it worked? (success criteria)

- Audience members share their appreciation of the show.
- The jury teams give positive feedback.
- New members sign up for next year.
