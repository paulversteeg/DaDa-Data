# User research (baseline)

Research that holds for the whole platform: users, context of use, customer value. Summaries only, with no names or anything that identifies a participant. These are the team's own accounts of feedback, not formal research.

## Findings

| Finding | Source | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| Last season's feedback said the programme might be too easy for the members. In performance it turned out to be the opposite. | Feedback on last season's programme | Don't rely on this kind of feedback about difficulty. Check it against what happens in rehearsal. |
| Former members were surprised by the level of the programme. | Conversations with former members | The unit used to compete at a higher level in the circuit. Members are now younger and less experienced, so the unit deliberately moved to the A Class. That step back is part of the plan. Some people may disagree. |

## Context

- Last season was a test year, and the unit started relatively late.
- This season is a deliberate step up. The aim is to grow again in the number of members, the health of the unit, how enthusiastic people are, and the professionalism of the programme.
- The programme is already further along than last year. The first part is done.
