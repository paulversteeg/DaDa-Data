# Glossary

Domain terms and abbreviations, plus kit terms (gate, DD-XXX).

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Glitch | The name of a main effect in the show. |
| Carrier | The harness that members use to carry the drums. |
| Gymzaal (gym hall) | A gym hall is needed to rehearse the drill. The planning sheet calls it "zaal". |
| JMC | The clubhouse of Jubal. It is only partly suitable for visual rehearsals: movement and music subsectionals and ensemble rehearsals can be done there, but no drill. |
| Front ensemble (FE) | The musical performers at the front of the field with large instruments: drumkit, vibraphone, marimba, glockenspiel, synthesizer. |
| Soundscape | The sound design layer, made of samples and synth sounds. It is played by the synth player. |
| Dot | A member's coordinate in the field, as part of a form. In the visual design, say "polka dot" instead, to avoid confusion. |
| Polka dot | The dot as a visual element in the design, such as the dot-grid motif. Not a member's position (that is a dot). |
| Caption | A judged part of the show, each with its own jury member: Music, Effect-Music, Effect-Visual and Visual. Timing & Penalty is a separate jury member who only gives penalties. |
| Box | One of five levels (1 to 5) in the jury's criteria reference. A score falls in the box that applies most of the time during the performance. |
| Junior Class | The CGN age class for members under 17 on 1 April. DaDa Data does not compete in it. The "junior layer" in the show description is a different thing: the playful, absurd layer for the younger members. |
