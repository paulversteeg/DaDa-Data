# Brand guidelines

Identity, visual language and how the brand shows up in the product.

Source: the visual identity one-pager "Mens vs. Machine / Dada" (dated 18-09-2026), based on the visual briefing by Peter Franken of 30-07-2026. This is the approved identity. The colour palette is in `design-system/DESIGN.md`.

## Idea

From a strict grid, via collision and chaos, to a human synthesis.

## Four contrasts

The look moves between these pairs, the same way the show moves from machine to human:

- Grid to chaos
- Black and white to colour
- Small and uniform to large and rough
- Digital to handmade

## Core motif

In this project "dot" means a member's position in the field, so the design element is always called a polka dot (see `history/glossary.md`).

A polka-dot grid that becomes organic. On the system side it is tiny, even black polka dots. Towards the human / Dada side the polka dots grow, scatter and turn colourful.

## Signature effects

- **Chromatic aberration:** a red/green glitch edge on contours. The system makes a mistake.
- **Graffiti fill:** a raw, hand-sprayed texture used as the fill in the human part.

## Applications

**Uniforms**

- Grid print on the front panel
- Colour gradient towards the back and sleeves
- Glitch edge on the collar or hem

**Props and floor**

- Floor: grid side to graffiti side. A large plastic floor mat is the only prop this season. If time and budget allow, it may be styled further with polka dots.
- Props on the field are out this season (see `history/dont-do.md`), so the one-pager's props ideas (AI props in black and white, props taken over in colour and graffiti) do not apply for now.

**Marketing**

- Image in five parts (4:5 teasers)
- A series that reveals the story
- Mono type for titles, handwriting for quotes

## Examples

The approved posters and banner use halftone polka dots, large orange "DADA" lettering, a binary-code background, and sound words (BOEM, PAUKE, SLAG) set in bold sans-serif and italic serif type.
