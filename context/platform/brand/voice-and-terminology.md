# Voice and terminology

## Tone of voice

The tone shifts with the four parts of the show:

- **The Algorithmic Prison:** cold, emotionless, robotic and perfect.
- **The Scream:** rebellious and uncomfortable, with tension and glitches.
- **The Wonder:** tender, vulnerable and quiet.
- **The Human Showcase:** raw, joyful and energetic, a celebration of creativity and showmanship.

Across the whole show, the junior layer is playful and absurd, and the senior layer carries the tension and the outsider-rock attitude. Everything is clear on the surface, with more depth for those who look closer.
